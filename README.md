## Why Phantom

- **Safe defaults.** Anything that spends gems, keys or speed-ups is off until you switch it on, and always has a limit.
- **Multiple accounts.** One BlueStacks instance per account; each gets its own feature list and schedule.
- **Progress dashboard.** Every account card shows what the bot is doing now and charts gems, power, bread and Town Center level since install.
- **Take over any time.** Press **F9** to pause and play yourself, **F10** to hand back.
- **No game files touched.** Phantom only reads the screen and taps through ADB. It never modifies the game or its memory.

## Download and install

1. Install **BlueStacks 5** and **Kingshot** from the Play Store inside it.
2. In BlueStacks: *Settings → Advanced → Android Debug Bridge (ADB)*: turn it **on**.
3. Download **Phantom_Setup.exe** from [Releases](../../releases/latest) and run it. No administrator rights are needed.
4. Open Phantom, click **+ Connect Account**, pick your BlueStacks instance and save.
5. Click **Connect** to check the connection (no automation yet), choose your features, then **Start**.

Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* for a new app. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## FAQ

**Is there a bot to play Kingshot?** Yes. Phantom is a Kingshot bot for Windows PC. It plays Kingshot inside BlueStacks: it claims daily rewards, runs Conquest and Intel missions, upgrades heroes and follows the chapter quests.

**Is this Kingshot bot free?** Yes. Every new PC gets a 15-day trial with Pro features (3 hours a day). After it, the free plan never expires: it runs the daily reward features for 1 hour of bot time per game day.

**What are the system requirements?** Windows 10 or 11 and BlueStacks 5 with ADB turned on. Each account runs in its own BlueStacks instance, so more accounts need more RAM.

**Does it work without BlueStacks?** Not yet. Phantom runs Kingshot in BlueStacks 5 on Windows. LDPlayer, MuMu and other emulators are not supported yet.

**Is there a Kingshot bot for Android or iPhone?** Phantom runs on a Windows PC only. It plays the Android version of Kingshot inside BlueStacks, so your phone stays free.

**Is Phantom a Kingshot macro or a bot?** A bot. A macro replays fixed taps; Phantom reads the screen, decides what to do next and recovers when a popup or an event gets in the way. That is what lets it auto farm Kingshot for hours without you watching.

**Can I get banned?** Any automation breaks most games' terms, and the publisher can act on accounts that use it. Phantom plays at human speed with random pauses, but no bot can promise zero risk.

**Does it need my game password?** No. It only sees what BlueStacks shows on screen.
